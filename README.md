# Emberglass — an HD-2D open world in Godot 4.6 (C#)

A small open world in the HD-2D style: pixel-art sprite characters standing in a lit, textured 3D world, seen from a low fixed camera. Almost everything in it is generated by code. The buildings, props, textures and townsfolk sprites come from Python scripts that drive Blender. The world layout, terrain, foliage, roads and city plan come from C# generators that bake into ordinary Godot scenes you can then edit by hand.

## What's in the world

- **Six regions on one continuous terrain**, no loading screens: woods, meadows, fen, crags, the **Hoarfells** (snow, with a frozen lake and a ship) and the **Ashdunes** (desert).
- **Emberglass**, a walled city with districts, terraces, stairs, squares, a market fountain, lamps and a sunset look.
- **Villages and townsfolk**: NPCs with faces and clothing detail walk set routes; you can talk to people and pet the animals.
- **Look stack**: Terrain3D ground with grass and stones, sprite trees in groves, mist, a colour grade, soft bloom, seasonal fog, FSR 2 upscaling. Foliage that blocks the player is ghosted whole rather than cut open.
- **Player**: 8-facing sprite baked at two camera angles, moved for feel rather than animation frames.

## Repository layout

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `game/` | The Godot 4.6 .NET project (`worldbuilder.csproj`, main scene `scenes/world.tscn`). About 23k lines of C#: world generation, terrain, city planning, foliage, townsfolk, fauna, weather, water, camera rig, post-processing. |
| `game/world/` | The baked, hand-editable world: one scene per site plus `layout.tscn`, `terrain.tscn` and `look/`. See `game/world/README.md` for what to edit where. |
| `game/prefabs/`, `game/assets/` | Every asset as a Godot prefab, plus the GLB models, textures and sprites they use. |
| `game/addons/terrain_3d/` | [Terrain3D](https://github.com/TokisanGames/Terrain3D) by Cory Petkovsek and Roope Palmroos, MIT licence (see its `LICENSE.txt`). |
| `scripts/` | About 39k lines of Python that run inside Blender: modular building kits (walls, roofs, towers, columns, paving), monuments, props, procedural materials, sprite baking at a fixed isometric rig, and `forge/`, the townsfolk and city-kit generators. |

## Run it

1. Install **Godot 4.6 .NET** (C# build).
2. Open `game/project.godot`. `game/NuGet.config` points at the GodotSharp packages bundled with Godot; change the path to your own Godot install.
3. Press **Build**, then **F5**.

**Controls:** WASD to move, Shift to dash and run, Space to jump, J or left click to attack, E to talk to people and pet animals, F1–F8 to travel between the built places, F11 to toggle haze and post-processing.

## Regenerating assets

The Blender scripts are run headless:

```bash
blender --background --python scripts/build_emberglass_kit.py
```

Generated GLBs and sprites land in `game/assets/`. Render outputs, `.blend` files and screenshots are not committed; the scripts recreate them.
